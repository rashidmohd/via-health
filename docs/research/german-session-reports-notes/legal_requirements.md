# Legal Requirements for Documenting Psychotherapy Sessions in Germany (state: Oct 2026)

Scope: Psychologische Psychotherapeut:innen (PP), Kinder- und Jugendlichenpsychotherapeut:innen (KJP), Fachpsychotherapeut:innen under the new law (all are Kammer members), and Heilpraktiker für Psychotherapie (HPP) where the rules differ.
Primary sources checked in this session: §§630f, 630g, 630h BGB, §§201, 203 StGB (gesetze-im-internet.de); full text of the BPtK Muster-Berufsordnung (MBO), last amended 15 Nov 2025; PKN (Niedersachsen) BO amendment of 25 Apr 2026; PTK NRW documentation recommendations (2020); PTK Berlin lecture on patient data (RAin Dittberner, 2015).

## 1. §630f BGB / §9 MBO: what must be documented, when, how, and for how long

### Takeaway
§630f BGB (in force since the Patientenrechtegesetz 2013) and the near-identical §9 MBO require a paper or electronic Patientenakte kept "in unmittelbarem zeitlichen Zusammenhang" with the treatment. Corrections must leave the original content and the date of the change visible, also in electronic records. The record must be kept for 10 years after treatment ends. The MBO extends the duty explicitly to counselling ("Behandlung oder Beratung").

### Cited Findings
- §630f Abs. 1 BGB: the treating party must keep a Patientenakte on paper or electronically, "in unmittelbarem zeitlichen Zusammenhang mit der Behandlung". Corrections and changes are allowed only if the original content stays recognizable and the date of the change is shown, and this also applies to electronic records. — [§630f BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630f.html)
- §630f Abs. 2 BGB (verbatim as quoted by PTK NRW): "Der Behandelnde ist verpflichtet, in der Patientenakte sämtliche aus fachlicher Sicht für die derzeitige und künftige Behandlung wesentlichen Maßnahmen und deren Ergebnisse aufzuzeichnen, insbesondere die Anamnese, Diagnosen, Untersuchungen, Untersuchungsergebnisse, Befunde, Therapien und ihre Wirkungen, Eingriffe und ihre Wirkungen, Einwilligungen und Aufklärungen. Arztbriefe sind in die Patientenakte aufzunehmen." — [PTK NRW, Empfehlungen zur Dokumentation (2020)](https://www.ptk-nrw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/meldungen/2020/Empfehlungen_Dok_Internet.pdf); [§630f BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630f.html)
- §630f Abs. 3 BGB: keep the record for 10 years after treatment ends, unless other laws set different periods. — [§630f BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630f.html)
- §9 Abs. 1 MBO (verbatim): Psychotherapists must "zum Zweck der Dokumentation in unmittelbarem zeitlichen Zusammenhang mit der Behandlung oder Beratung eine Patientenakte in Papierform oder elektronisch zu führen. Berichtigungen und Änderungen von Eintragungen in der Patientenakte sind nur zulässig, wenn neben dem ursprünglichen Inhalt erkennbar bleibt, wann sie vorgenommen worden sind. Dies ist auch für elektronisch geführte Patientenakten sicherzustellen." — [BPtK Muster-Berufsordnung, as amended 15.11.2025](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- §9 Abs. 2 MBO repeats the §630f Abs. 2 content list word for word ("sämtliche aus fachlicher Sicht ... wesentlichen Maßnahmen ... Arztbriefe sind in die Patientenakte aufzunehmen"). — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- §9 Abs. 3 MBO: records must be kept 10 years after treatment ends, unless statute requires longer. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- §10 MBO (Datensicherheit): psychotherapists must ensure that collected data "und persönliche Aufzeichnungen" are stored securely and fully protected against unauthorized access. Staff or third parties who process data must be bound to data protection rules. This also applies to electronically stored data, which must meet current security standards. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- §24 MBO (giving up a practice): on closing, sale, or death, data security must be ensured. Records go to a successor only with the patient's written consent. Otherwise the former owner keeps them, or the successor keeps them under lock, separate from the successor's own records. Records are destroyed after the §9 Abs. 3 retention period. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- PTK Berlin's interpretation: "zeitnah" means as a rule the same or the following day. Keyword-style documentation is enough. Entries need to be understandable to a professional, not to a layperson (abbreviations are fine). The retention duty does not end when the practice closes or the therapist dies. — [PTK Berlin, Dittberner lecture 17.11.2015](https://psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/media/727)
- Some Länder also set the duty in statute. In NRW, §30 Nr. 3 HeilBerG requires psychotherapists to "über in Ausübung ihres Berufs gemachte Feststellungen und getroffene Maßnahmen Aufzeichnungen zu fertigen". — [PTK NRW 2020](https://www.ptk-nrw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/meldungen/2020/Empfehlungen_Dok_Internet.pdf)
- Kammer minimum standard (PTK NRW) for each session (Verlaufsdokumentation). Mandatory every session: patient name, date, time, session number. Also as applicable: participants, location, session type, the topic at the patient's narrative level, the therapist's theory-level understanding (method-specific), interventions and reactions, changes in psychological findings (e.g. current suicide risk), changes in treatment progress, and new Aufklärung/Einwilligung when the approach changes. At the end of therapy: an Epikrise. The Basisdokumentation covers anamnesis, ICD diagnosis, indication, treatment plan, Aufklärung/Einwilligung with date and content, Schweigepflichtentbindungen, and similar items. — [PTK NRW 2020](https://www.ptk-nrw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/meldungen/2020/Empfehlungen_Dok_Internet.pdf)
- PTK NRW: it is not enough to record only what the patient said. "Vielmehr muss ein hypothesengeleitetes Behandlungskonzept erkennbar sein". The course of treatment need not be visible in every single entry, only across entries taken together. The Kammer recommends periodic entries on the case conceptualization. — [PTK NRW 2020](https://www.ptk-nrw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/meldungen/2020/Empfehlungen_Dok_Internet.pdf)
- Case law on the purpose of documentation (Berufsgericht für Heilberufe at VG Münster, 11.09.2013, 17 K 2564/11.T): documentation serves therapy planning, continuation by another therapist or a clinic, and the ability of the Kammer or a court to reconstruct the treatment. — [PTK NRW 2020](https://www.ptk-nrw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/meldungen/2020/Empfehlungen_Dok_Internet.pdf)
- §11 Abs. 6 MBO and new §11 Abs. 4 BO PKN: the inspection rules do not apply to data in the ePA under §341 SGB V. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf); [PKN BO-Änderung 25.04.2026](https://www.pknds.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/20260427_Bekanntmachung_Berufsordnung.pdf)
- ePA (since 2025): psychotherapists must fill the ePA only with data they collected themselves in the current treatment, that exist electronically, and to which the patient has not objected. KBV's example is the Befundbericht from the Psychotherapeutische Sprechstunde. For sensitive data, including mental illness, they must tell the patient about the right to object (§347 Abs. 3 S. 6 SGB V). An objection must be recorded in the treatment documentation. — [KBV Praxisnachricht 14.08.2025](https://www.kbv.de/praxis/tools-und-services/praxisnachrichten/2025/08-14/alles-nur-eine-frage-wie-nutzen-psychotherapeuten-die-epa); [KVSA PRO 12/2024](https://www.kvsa.de/fileadmin/user_upload/PDF/Praxis/IT_in_der_Praxis/PRO_12-2024_ePA.pdf); [KBV PraxisInfoSpezial ePA Rechtsfragen](https://kbv.de/media/sp/PraxisInfoSpezial_ePA_Rechtsfragen.pdf)

### Inferences
- Session notes are not optional. Every session needs at least date, time, and session number, plus the essential content and interventions. A product that drafts notes should produce entries that are clearly dated, final, and versioned.
- Any editing feature must be append-only or keep a visible audit trail: the original content stays recognizable and each change carries a timestamp. Silently overwriting a finalized note breaches §630f Abs. 1 S. 2–3 BGB and §9 Abs. 1 MBO.
- The ePA is a separate legal object. Session notes do not go into the ePA by default; the fill duty covers documents such as Befundberichte and Arztbriefe.

### Gaps
- I did not verify the individual Landeskammer BO texts for Bayern, Berlin, or NRW for small deviations from §9 MBO. Per the BPtK, the MBO is a model that the Landeskammern adopt; Niedersachsen's 2026 version matches MBO §11.
- I found no explicit court ruling defining "unmittelbarer zeitlicher Zusammenhang" for psychotherapy. The "same or next day" reading comes from a Kammer lawyer's slide.

## 2. §630g BGB / §11 MBO: the patient's right to inspect, exceptions, copies, case law, GDPR Art. 15

### Takeaway
Patients may inspect their complete record without delay. The first copy has been free since an amendment to §630g that took effect 6 Feb 2026, following EuGH C-307/22. Inspection can be refused only for "erhebliche therapeutische Gründe" or "erhebliche Rechte Dritter", and the refusal must be justified. Under the Kammer rules, the therapist's subjective impressions written into the record are in principle open to inspection. A narrow exception allows withholding individual entries that reveal the therapist's own personality, after a balancing test that the Kammer can review.

### Cited Findings
- §630g Abs. 1 BGB (current version): patients may demand prompt inspection of the complete Patientenakte; §811 BGB applies. They may demand copies, including electronic ones, and the first copy is free. — [§630g BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630g.html)
- §630g Abs. 2: inspection may be refused only where "erhebliche therapeutische Gründe oder sonstige erhebliche Rechte Dritter" stand against it, and the refusal must be justified. — [§630g BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630g.html)
- §630g Abs. 3: heirs may exercise the right for property interests and must pay the costs. Next of kin may exercise it for non-material interests. Both are excluded if the patient's express or presumed will is against it. — [§630g BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630g.html)
- §630g Abs. 4 (new): the data subject's data protection rights remain unaffected. Where data protection law requires free access, no fee may be charged under Abs. 1. The refusal grounds of Abs. 2 also apply to data protection claims between therapist and patient. — [§630g BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630g.html)
- EuGH 26.10.2023, C-307/22 (referred by the BGH, dental patient): under Art. 12 Abs. 5 and Art. 15 Abs. 1 and 3 GDPR, the patient is entitled to a first copy free of charge and without giving reasons. Member states may not deviate from this, even to protect the providers' economic interests. The right covers all documents in the record needed to understand the personal data. — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/EuGH-C30722-Vorlage-Patientenakte-Kopie-Datenschutzgrundverordnung-DSGVO-Vorabentscheidungsverfahren-BGH); [beck-aktuell](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtsprechung/eugh-patientenakte-patient-hat-recht-auf-unentgeltliche-erste-kopie-2023-10-26); [otto-schmidt](https://www.otto-schmidt.de/news/zivil-und-zivilverfahrensrecht/recht-auf-unentgeltliche-kopie-der-medizinischen-akte-2023-10-27.html)
- The §630g amendment came through a law of 3 Feb 2026 (BGBl. 2026 I Nr. 28), in force 6 Feb 2026. The BMJ draft dates from May 2024. These details come from secondary sources (buzer.de, iww.de), but the current gesetze-im-internet text matches them. — [buzer.de](https://www.buzer.de/gesetz/6597/al179750.htm); [iww.de](https://www.iww.de/pp/recht/akteneinsicht-neuer-630g-bgb-definiert-recht-auf-kostenlose-erstkopie-der-patientenakte-f172545); [§630g BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630g.html)
- §11 Abs. 1 MBO (verbatim): "Auch persönliche Eindrücke und subjektive Wahrnehmungen des*der Psychotherapeut*in, die gemäß § 9 in der Patientenakte dokumentiert worden sind, unterliegen grundsätzlich dem Einsichtsrecht des*der Patient*in." Inspection covers the record "in ihrer Originalfassung", and the first copy or electronic transcript is free. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- §11 Abs. 2 MBO: a further copy may be charged if the record is unchanged and the request comes within 3 months of the last copy. Manifestly unfounded or excessive requests may be refused or charged. The decision must be justified and documented. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- §11 Abs. 3 MBO: refusal in whole or part is allowed only on the §630g grounds. If the therapist exceptionally withholds individual entries "weil diese Einblick in seine*ihre Persönlichkeit geben und ihre Offenlegung sein*ihr Persönlichkeitsrecht berührt", this is not a professional violation if the therapist's interest outweighs the patient's in a balancing test. Any refusal must be justified to the patient. Under §11 Abs. 4, the Landeskammer may demand to see the withheld entries to review the decision. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- Niedersachsen adopted the same wording in its BO amendment of 25.04.2026 (§11 Abs. 1–2 BO PKN), adding new Abs. 4 that excludes ePA data. — [PKN Bekanntmachung 2026](https://www.pknds.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/20260427_Bekanntmachung_Berufsordnung.pdf)
- Case law:
  - BVerfG, 16.09.1998, 1 BvR 1130/98: inspection covers "grundsätzlich nur Aufzeichnungen über objektive physische Befunde und Berichte über Behandlungsmaßnahmen", but in individual cases may extend to non-objectified findings. PTK Berlin's comment: "§630g BGB macht einen Regelfall daraus!"
  - BVerfG, 09.01.2006, 2 BvR 443/02 (Maßregelvollzug): a further opening toward patients' personality rights.
  - BGH, 07.11.2013, III ZR 54/13: a right to inspect training-analysis records (Lehranalyse), with the analyst allowed to redact where the analyst's own personality rights are affected.
  - Older BGH case law (BGHZ 85, 327; BGHZ 106, 146) excluded subjective notes; §630g reversed this.
  - Sources: [PTK Berlin lecture](https://psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/media/727); [dr-datenschutz.de](https://www.dr-datenschutz.de/die-gedanken-sind-frei-die-einsicht-in-akten-leider-auch/); search snippets citing the BGHZ references at [psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/media/333](https://psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/media/333)
- PTK Berlin's practical reading: valid therapeutic grounds include concrete self-harm or suicide risk. Valid third-party rights include sensitive information about the patient's parents given only to the therapist. A blanket refusal is not allowed. Milder means (accompanied inspection, partial inspection) must be considered first. Inspection must be granted "unverzüglich" (§121 BGB), as a rule where the records are kept. — [PTK Berlin lecture](https://psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/media/727)
- A commentator (Thorwart, Psychotherapeutenjournal 1/2014) raised constitutional concerns about the lack of a refusal option for PP/KJP. — [PTK Berlin lecture](https://psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/media/727)

### Inferences
- "Persönliche Aufzeichnungen" versus Patientenakte: once impressions are written into the record, they are presumptively open to inspection. There is no recognized category of a separate, inspection-free "Nebenakte" for subjective notes. §10 MBO mentions "persönliche Aufzeichnungen" only for data security, and the GDPR Art. 15 right attaches to any personal data about the patient wherever it is stored. Only the narrow personality-rights exception in §11 Abs. 3 S. 2 MBO remains, and the Kammer can review it.
- Anything a tool stores about a client, including AI drafts, transcripts, and summaries, should be assumed accessible to the client under Art. 15 GDPR and possibly §630g. The design must support full export of a client's data and redaction with documented reasons.

### Gaps
- I found no post-2013 BGH ruling that applies §630g specifically to psychotherapist process notes. I could not confirm a "2 BvR 2462/13" decision.

## 3. Audio/video recording of sessions: consent, §201 StGB, and status as part of the Patientenakte

### Takeaway
Recording is lawful only with the patient's prior consent. §8 Abs. 7 MBO says so expressly, requires the patient to be told of a right to demand deletion, and puts use of the recording under the duty of confidentiality. Recording the non-public spoken word without authorization is a crime under §201 StGB (up to 3 years). No source found settles whether raw recordings or transcripts must be kept as part of the 10-year Patientenakte.

### Cited Findings
- §8 Abs. 7 MBO (verbatim): "Ton- und Bildaufnahmen psychotherapeutischer Tätigkeit bedürfen der vorherigen Einwilligung des*der Patient*in. Ihre Verwendung unterliegt der Schweigepflicht. Der*Die Patient*in ist über das Recht zu informieren, eine Löschung zu verlangen." — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- §201 Abs. 1 StGB: whoever "unbefugt" makes a sound recording of "das nichtöffentlich gesprochene Wort eines anderen", or uses it or makes it available to a third party, faces "Freiheitsstrafe bis zu drei Jahren oder mit Geldstrafe". Abs. 2 covers eavesdropping with a listening device and public disclosure. — [§201 StGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stgb/__201.html)
- PTK Berlin's patient information: recordings of sessions by video or tape (e.g. for quality assurance) require the patient's consent. — [PTK Berlin, Patientenrechte](https://psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/patientenrechte)
- §8 Abs. 6 MBO: supervision, intervision, research, and teaching may use only anonymized information. If anonymity cannot be guaranteed, disclosure requires an express prior release from confidentiality. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- Practice example: the IGPP consent form asks separately for each purpose (intervision, supervision, training). — [IGPP Einwilligung](https://igpp.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Einwilligung_Audio-Videoaufnahmen.pdf)
- For comparison, Austrian law requires prior written consent for recordings; German MBO does not require written form. — [RIS Österreich](https://ris.bka.gv.at/Dokumente/Bundesnormen/NOR40261815/NOR40261815.pdf)
- A 2026 Verhaltenstherapie (Karger) article finds that available AI transcription tools are often unsuitable for psychotherapy because data protection and reliability are not guaranteed, and proposes local, air-gapped processing. — [Karger, Verhaltenstherapie 36(2):91](https://karger.com/ver/article-split/36/2/91/939843/Language-Model-bitte-zum-Diktat-Transkription)

### Inferences
- The patient's consent, given before the session, makes the recording "befugt" under §201 StGB. All participants must consent, including in couple, family, or group sessions, and for KJP the Sorgeberechtigte together with a child who has capacity to consent. Consent should name the purpose (documentation drafting via AI), the processor, the retention or deletion period, and the right to demand deletion (§8 Abs. 7 MBO). Written or text form is advisable for proof, though not legally required by the MBO.
- Patientenakte status: §630f requires recording what is "wesentlich". It does not require keeping verbatim recordings. A recording used only as a transient aid for drafting the therapist's note, and deleted promptly, is arguably not part of the Akte. Its essential content lives on in the finalized note. Once stored, however, a recording or transcript is personal data subject to Art. 15 GDPR while it exists. The §8 Abs. 7 MBO deletion right also points toward recordings not being a mandatory 10-year retention object. This is my reading; it is not confirmed by any Kammer source.
- If a transcript is kept and treated as part of the record (e.g. a Befund is based on it), it falls under §630g inspection and the 10-year retention.

### Gaps
- I found no Landeskammer Praxis-Info or court decision that explicitly says whether session recordings or transcripts must enter the Patientenakte. This should be raised with a Kammer or a medical-law lawyer.
- I found no specific Kammer guidance on AI transcription. A blog attributes a "BPtK Praxisinformation" on KI to the BPtK (checkpoints: EU AI Act, MDR, §203 StGB), but I could not verify it on bptk.de. BPtK documents I could verify: the 2023 DPT resolution "Keine vorschnelle Einführung von KI-Anwendungen!" — [BPtK Resolution](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Resolution_Keine_vorschnelle_Einfuehrung_von_KI_Anwendungen_5e55d24b88.pdf) — and a Digitalisierung curriculum updated on 13.02.2026 with KI content — [BPtK Curriculum KI-Update](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Curriculum_Digitalisierung_und_ihre_Anwendungen_in_der_Psychotherapie_Erweiterung_KI_Update_028ad89dec.pdf).

## 4. §203 StGB confidentiality and external service providers (cloud/AI)

### Takeaway
PP and KJP are professional secret-holders under §203 Abs. 1 StGB. Since the 2017 reform, §203 Abs. 3 S. 2 allows them to disclose secrets to "sonstige mitwirkende Personen", such as IT, cloud, or transcription providers, to the extent necessary. In return, §203 Abs. 4 makes the therapist liable if they fail to ensure the provider, and its subcontractors, are bound to secrecy. A GDPR Art. 28 processing agreement (AVV) alone does not cover this criminal-law layer.

### Cited Findings
- PTK Berlin: PP/KJP are Berufsgeheimnisträger under §203 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 StGB. The offence requires intent and is prosecuted only on complaint (§205 StGB). The professional confidentiality clause (§8 Abs. 2 BO) does not justify a disclosure under criminal law. — [PTK Berlin lecture](https://psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/media/727)
- §203 Abs. 3 StGB: passing secrets to employed assistants or trainees is not an "Offenbaren". Under S. 2, secret-holders may disclose to "sonstige Personen ... die an ihrer beruflichen oder dienstlichen Tätigkeit mitwirken", insofar as necessary for using their services. This extends to further persons those contributors use. — [§203 StGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stgb/__203.html)
- §203 Abs. 4 StGB: a contributing person who discloses without authorization faces up to 1 year or a fine. The secret-holder is also liable if a contributing person discloses and the secret-holder "nicht dafür Sorge getragen hat", meaning did not ensure that person was bound to secrecy. The same applies down the subcontractor chain. — [§203 StGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stgb/__203.html)
- §8 Abs. 5 MBO: assistants, trainees, and "sonstigen Personen, die an der beruflichen ... Tätigkeit ... mitwirken" must be bound to confidentiality, "Dies ist schriftlich festzuhalten." §10 Abs. 1 MBO requires third parties who process data to be bound to data protection rules. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- Bitkom offers a model §203 confidentiality undertaking: the provider's staff and subcontractors commit in text form and are instructed on §203 Abs. 4. — [Bitkom Muster §203 StGB (2018)](https://www.bitkom.org/sites/main/files/file/import/20180718-Muster-203StGB-final.pdf)
- §8 Abs. 2 MBO: disclosure is allowed only with a valid release, a statutory permission, or to protect a higher-ranking legal interest. §8 Abs. 8 limits disclosure to the information needed in the individual case. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)

### Inferences
- An AI or cloud documentation provider can be a "mitwirkende Person" when its service is necessary for the therapist's work (documentation is), so no patient release is required under §203. Conditions:
  1. The provider is contractually bound to confidentiality, in writing or text form, with an instruction on §203 Abs. 4.
  2. The same applies to subprocessors such as LLM or ASR vendors.
  3. Disclosure is limited to what is necessary.
  4. The provider is carefully selected and monitored.
- Practical vendor checklist: a §203 Verschwiegenheitsverpflichtung in addition to the AVV; flow-down to all subprocessors; data minimization (pseudonymization where possible); no use for model training; documented deletion.
- Disclosure abroad: §203 does not forbid it as such, but GDPR Chapter V transfer rules apply. Where the "mitwirkende" framework applies abroad is debated in the literature (not verified here).

### Gaps
- I found no court decision applying §203 Abs. 3 S. 2 to LLM or AI transcription providers. The secondary sources were vendor blogs.

## 5. GDPR Art. 9, §22 BDSG, Art. 28 processors, DSFA

### Takeaway
Session content is health data (Art. 9 GDPR). A practice processes it under Art. 9 Abs. 2 lit. h with Abs. 3 (professional secrecy), together with §22 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 lit. b BDSG, and AI vendors must be bound by an Art. 28 AVV. A DSFA is mandatory for "umfangreiche" processing of health data. For a single-handed practice that threshold is usually not met (Erwägungsgrund 91), but a new technology such as AI transcription can independently trigger it. A vendor processing many practices' data almost certainly needs its own DSFA.

### Cited Findings
- The DSK published a Muss-Liste (Art. 35 Abs. 4 GDPR) of processing that always requires a DSFA, including large-scale processing of health data and merging data from different sources. — [Rödl & Partner, 27.11.2025](https://www.roedl.com/insights/datenschutz-folgenabschaetzung-dsfa-im-krankenhaus/)
- The Bavarian DPA (BayLDA): for "die normale Arztpraxis" the "umfangreich" threshold is as a rule not met (in the context of the DPO and DSFA duty). — [BayLDA FAQ DSB im medizinischen Bereich](https://www.lda.bayern.de/media/FAQ_DSB_im_medizinischen_Bereich.pdf)
- The Ärztekammer Westfalen-Lippe also says "umfangreich" depends on the individual case. — [ÄKWL Merkblatt DSFA](https://www.aekwl.de/fileadmin/allgemein/dsgvo/Merkblatt_Datenschutzfolgeabschaetzung_Stand_07.02.2018.pdf)
- §10 MBO requires data security in line with "jeweils aktuellen Sicherheitsstandards". — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)

### Inferences (statutory content from my own knowledge of the text; I did not fetch these norms in this session)
- Art. 9 Abs. 2 lit. h and Abs. 3 GDPR allow processing for health care by persons under professional secrecy. Mirrored in §22 Abs. 1 Nr. 1 lit. b BDSG, which also requires "angemessene und spezifische Maßnahmen" under §22 Abs. 2 BDSG (e.g. access control, logging, pseudonymization, encryption). Treatment documentation therefore does not need consent as its legal basis. Recording and AI processing beyond what is necessary are, in practice, based on explicit consent (Art. 9 Abs. 2 lit. a) to be safe, which also serves as the §201 StGB authorization. — [Art. 9 DSGVO](https://dsgvo-gesetz.de/art-9-dsgvo/); [§22 BDSG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bdsg_2018/__22.html)
- Art. 28 GDPR: the AI provider is a processor and needs an AVV listing subprocessors. Art. 35 Abs. 1 GDPR ("neue Technologien", high risk) is likely met by AI transcription of therapy sessions, so a DSFA is advisable even for small practices. A vendor should provide a template DSFA. — [Art. 35 DSGVO](https://dsgvo-gesetz.de/art-35-dsgvo/)

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the DSK Muss-Liste text directly, nor any DSK statement on AI transcription in health care.

## 6. Authorship: must the therapist personally write the documentation? Can an assistant or software draft it?

### Takeaway
No statute or Kammer rule found requires the therapist to type the record personally, and none requires a signature on each entry. But documentation is the treating party's duty ("der Behandelnde"), the therapist "behandelt persönlich und eigenverantwortlich", and delegation leaves overall responsibility with the therapist. A draft by an assistant or AI is acceptable only if the therapist reviews it and adopts it as their own.

### Cited Findings
- §630f BGB puts the duty on "der Behandelnde". — [§630f BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630f.html)
- New §5 Abs. 1 BO PKN (2026): "Psychotherapeutinnen und Psychotherapeuten behandeln persönlich und eigenverantwortlich." — [PKN BO-Änderung 2026](https://www.pknds.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/20260427_Bekanntmachung_Berufsordnung.pdf)
- §18 MBO: diagnostic sub-tasks and supplementary measures may be delegated to qualified third parties with the patient's consent. Overall responsibility remains with the delegating psychotherapist, who must check the delegated work regularly. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- §8 Abs. 5 MBO allows assistants ("Gehilf*innen") to work on patient matters if bound to confidentiality in writing. — [BPtK MBO](https://api.bptk.de/uploads/Muster_Berufsordnung_der_B_Pt_K_412a6bcb36.pdf)
- PTK NRW: the documentation must reflect the therapist's hypothesis-driven treatment concept ("hypothesengeleitetes Behandlungskonzept"), not merely what the patient said. — [PTK NRW 2020](https://www.ptk-nrw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/meldungen/2020/Empfehlungen_Dok_Internet.pdf)

### Inferences
- An AI-generated note becomes the therapist's documentation only through review, editing, and approval. A mere transcript or summary would also not meet the NRW expectation of a recognizable clinical rationale. The system should log who approved each entry and when; this also supports the §630f Abs. 1 rule that changes must be traceable.
- No qualified electronic signature is required for the Patientenakte under §630f. Authenticity and integrity matter for evidentiary weight.

### Gaps
- I found no explicit Kammer statement on AI-drafted session notes or on signature or authorship requirements.

## 7. Liability: §630h Abs. 3 BGB presumption when documentation is missing

### Takeaway
If a medically required essential measure and its result are not recorded, or the record is not kept, §630h Abs. 3 BGB presumes that the measure was not taken. In a malpractice claim this shifts the burden of proof to the therapist ("Was nicht dokumentiert ist, ist nicht gemacht").

### Cited Findings
- §630h Abs. 3 BGB: if the treating party did not record a "medizinisch gebotene wesentliche Maßnahme und ihr Ergebnis" contrary to §630f Abs. 1 or 2, or did not keep the record contrary to §630f Abs. 3, "wird vermutet, dass er diese Maßnahme nicht getroffen hat". — [§630h BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630h.html)
- §630h Abs. 4: if the treating party lacked the qualification ("nicht befähigt") for the treatment, the lack is presumed to have caused the injury. — [§630h BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630h.html)
- PTK Berlin: errors and gaps lead to a presumption against the treating party (§630h Abs. 3). — [PTK Berlin lecture](https://psychotherapeutenkammer-berlin.de/media/727)
- Commentary (Stellpflug/Berns, MBO-Kommentar Rn. 370/371, quoted by PTK NRW): documentation serves accountability and evidence, "Was nicht dokumentiert ist, ist nicht gemacht." — [PTK NRW 2020](https://www.ptk-nrw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/meldungen/2020/Empfehlungen_Dok_Internet.pdf)

### Inferences
- Key items for psychotherapy include suicide risk assessment and resulting measures, Aufklärung and Einwilligung, and crisis interventions. If AI drafts omit them and the therapist does not add them, the therapist bears the liability risk. Templates should prompt for these fields.
- Records that can be changed without a trace lose evidentiary value. Courts may then disregard the record, which strengthens the case for an audit trail.

### Gaps
- I did not research specific psychotherapy malpractice rulings applying §630h Abs. 3.

## 8. Heilpraktiker für Psychotherapie (HPP): differences

### Takeaway
HPP are not Kammer members, so the MBO (§§8, 9, 11, including the recording rule) does not bind them. §§630a ff. BGB do apply to their treatment contracts, which means documentation, inspection, and 10-year retention apply in the same way. Whether §203 StGB covers them is doubtful: they are generally considered outside §203 and bound by contractual confidentiality, while §201 StGB and the GDPR apply fully.

### Cited Findings
- DeGPT overview: for PP/KJP and physicians, confidentiality is strictly regulated by §203 StGB and the Berufsordnung. For Heilpraktiker it arises from §630a ff. BGB, not from criminal law. HPP have no Kammer, and supervision lies with the Gesundheitsämter. — [DeGPT Information zur Ausbildung und Ausübung von Psychotherapie (29.6.25)](https://www.degpt.de/fileadmin/all_user/download/hilfe-fuer-betroffene/information_zur_ausbildung_und__aus%C3%BCbung_von_psychotherapie_29_6_25.pdf)
- §630f/§630g/§630h address "der Behandelnde", not only licensed professionals. — [§630f BGB](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgb/__630f.html)

### Inferences
- A product serving HPP should apply the same documentation, audit-trail, and inspection features (BGB), but cannot rely on the §203 Abs. 3 "mitwirkende Person" mechanism in the same way. GDPR Art. 9 still applies, and a release from confidentiality or explicit consent is the safer basis for disclosure to vendors. HPP associations (e.g. VFP, BDH) have voluntary codes; I did not verify their content.

### Gaps
- I found no authoritative commentary confirming whether HPP fall under §203 Abs. 1 StGB. I could not check the BDH or VFP Berufsordnung texts.
